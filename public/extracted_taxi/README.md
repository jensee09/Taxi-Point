# Rajkot Taxi Point: one-page taxi booking site (HTML, CSS, JS in one file)

## Before you publish (important)
1. In index.html, find and replace **91XXXXXXXXXX** with your number, e.g. 919876543210 (country code + number, no + or spaces).
2. Find and replace **+91 XXXXX XXXXX** with how you want the number shown, e.g. +91 98765 43210.
3. Replace **https://www.example.com/** (also in sitemap.xml and robots.txt) with your real website address.
4. Replace the brand name "Rajkot Taxi Point" if you use another name. Add your office address and email in the Contact section.
5. Add your real car photos to the images/ folder (see images/README.txt).
6. Check every claim (24x7, clean cars, drivers, airport service) is true for your business. Add your fares only if you want them public.

## Booking
No forms. Every button opens a phone call (tel:) or a WhatsApp chat with a ready message (wa.me).

## SEO included
Keyword title and description, one H1, local business + FAQ structured data (schema.org), canonical link, Open Graph, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, Gujarati and Hindi keywords, route and airport sections, fast single-file page.
After upload: add your site to Google Search Console and submit sitemap.xml, and create a free Google Business Profile for Rajkot (the biggest factor for "taxi in Rajkot" searches).
